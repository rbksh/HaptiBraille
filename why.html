Chapter 4 of 8: Why we do it

Why we're doing this

Because reading your own messages, privately and independently, shouldn't depend on your senses or your bank balance.

  • Privacy shouldn't depend on your senses.

    Sighted people read their messages silently. Blind and deafblind people deserve the same: to read a bank code, a medical result or a private message without anyone else knowing what it says.

  • Cost shouldn't decide who gets to read.

    Almost everyone already has a smartphone, and every smartphone already has a vibration motor. Software costs nothing extra to deliver. A reading tool that requires a device worth thousands of dollars leaves most people out.

  • Independence is built from small daily things.

    Reading your own messages, understanding a stranger at a counter, checking when the bus arrives. Each one done without help adds up to a more independent life.

  • Nobody should be left out of a conversation.

    Deafblind people are often cut off from the people around them simply because there's no shared channel. A phone that turns speech into touch opens one.

  • It's a skill worth teaching.

    Only about 1 in 10 blind and deafblind people can read vibration patterns today. That isn't because the others can't learn. It's because nobody has made it easy to. We want learning to take minutes a day, on a device people already own.
